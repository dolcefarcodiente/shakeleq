Portrait commissions

A painted portrait is made slowly, from life where possible, from photographs where not. Head studies, half-figures and full figures, in oil on canvas or charcoal on paper.

Request a portrait
  1. 01

    Conversation

    We talk about who the portrait is for, the size, and the room it will live in.

  2. 02

    Sitting or photographs

    One or two sittings in the Milan studio, or a set of photographs if you are elsewhere.

  3. 03

    Sketch

    A drawn study is sent for approval before paint touches canvas.

  4. 04

    Painting

    Four to eight weeks depending on size. Progress photographs along the way.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Collected from the studio, or packed and shipped insured anywhere in the world.

About

Janibek Shakeleq is a painter from Kazakhstan living and working in Milan. He studied painting at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Brera, where he completed his bachelor's degree.

His paintings return to the same figure: a body with its arms raised, hair and limbs turning into branches, set against the flat colours of the steppe and the studio wall. The work moves between oil on canvas, charcoal, and small studies on paper made from life.

He takes a limited number of portrait commissions each year and shows work in Milan and abroad.
